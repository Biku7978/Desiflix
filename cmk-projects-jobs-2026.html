CMK Projects Recruitment 2026

CMK Projects (P) Ltd. has announced hiring opportunities for experienced professionals in the construction and infrastructure sector. The company is looking for candidates who can contribute their technical knowledge, project experience and professional skills across different project functions. The current requirements cover senior engineering, quantity surveying, planning, quality control, MEP, safety, formwork and land surveying responsibilities.

These vacancies are suitable for professionals who have established experience in construction projects and are looking for their next career opportunity. Candidates with relevant experience can apply for the positions according to their qualifications, technical background and previous project responsibilities. The recruitment includes positions ranging from Project Manager and Senior Engineer roles to specialised technical and site-based positions.

The announced site locations are in Rampur, Bhuvanagiri District, Telangana and Donnimala, Bellary District, Karnataka. Applicants should carefully review the location requirements before sending their resumes. Since these are project-based construction positions, candidates should be prepared to work according to site requirements and project schedules.

CMK Projects has listed opportunities for professionals with experience in project management, QS and client billing, planning, QA and QC, MEP coordination, safety management, Mivan formwork, and land surveying. The required experience starts from 10 years for most listed positions, while the Project Manager position requires 18+ years of experience.

Candidates should submit an updated resume highlighting their complete work experience, current designation, previous projects, educational qualifications, technical skills and contact information. A clear and updated CV can help the recruitment team understand the applicant's professional background and suitability for the available position.

Open Positions

1. Project Manager

Experience: 18+ Years

The Project Manager position is intended for highly experienced construction professionals who can take responsibility for overall project execution and coordination. Candidates should have strong experience managing project activities, coordinating engineering teams, monitoring schedules, handling site requirements and communicating with project stakeholders.

2. Senior Engineer – QS & Client Billing

Experience: 10+ Years

This position is suitable for professionals with extensive experience in quantity surveying and client billing. Candidates should be familiar with measurement, quantity preparation, billing documentation, project records, work certification and coordination between site and commercial teams.

3. Senior Engineer – Planning

Experience: 10+ Years

The Senior Engineer – Planning role requires experienced planning professionals who can support project scheduling, progress monitoring and coordination of planned versus actual work. Candidates with strong construction planning experience can apply.

4. Senior Engineer – QA & QC

Experience: 10+ Years

The QA & QC position is designed for experienced quality professionals responsible for maintaining construction quality and ensuring work is completed according to applicable project specifications and quality procedures. Candidates should have practical site experience in quality inspection and documentation.

5. Senior Engineer – MEP

Experience: 10+ Years

The Senior Engineer – MEP position is suitable for experienced professionals with knowledge of mechanical, electrical and plumbing coordination in construction projects. Candidates should have strong site coordination skills and practical experience handling MEP-related activities.

6. Senior Officer – Safety

Experience: 10+ Years

The Senior Officer – Safety role is for experienced construction safety professionals. Candidates should have practical knowledge of site safety procedures, inspections, toolbox meetings, safety documentation, risk identification and coordination with project teams.

7. Mivan Formen

Experience: 10+ Years

Experienced Mivan formwork professionals are required for project site activities. Candidates with practical experience in Mivan formwork, aluminium formwork systems, site execution, alignment, checking and coordination can apply for this position.

8. Land Surveyor

Experience: 10+ Years

The Land Surveyor position requires experienced professionals who can handle construction surveying activities. Candidates should have strong knowledge of site measurements, levels, alignment, setting out and other surveying responsibilities related to construction projects.
