Mantra Properties Walk-In Drive 2026

Mantra Properties has announced a Walk-In Drive for experienced engineering and construction professionals in Mumbai. The recruitment drive is scheduled for 21st September 2026 (Monday) and candidates with relevant experience from civil contracting firms are eligible to attend.

The recruitment drive includes multiple departments covering construction execution, project planning, cost management, quality, purchase, billing, safety, stores and architectural functions. The available positions range from Engineer and Executive-level opportunities to Manager, Associate Manager, Senior Manager and Project Manager-level roles.

This recruitment drive can be relevant for experienced professionals who have already worked in the construction or civil contracting sector and are looking for opportunities in Mumbai. Candidates should carefully check the experience requirement and designation range of the position before attending the interview.

Department-Wise Career Opportunities

The Mantra Properties recruitment drive covers several important functions involved in construction and property development. Execution professionals may work closely with project teams and site activities, while planning professionals support project schedules and coordination. Cost Management and Billing positions are relevant for professionals working with project quantities, costs, measurements and commercial documentation.

Quality and Safety are also important functions on construction projects. Quality professionals generally support quality-related site processes, inspections and documentation, while safety teams focus on maintaining safe working practices and compliance at project locations. Store professionals support material management and site inventory operations.

The recruitment drive also includes architectural positions. Liaisoning Architect, Architectural Concept and Architectural Operations roles provide opportunities for professionals with experience in different architectural functions. The designation range for these roles varies from Executive and Senior Engineer to Associate Manager and Manager.
