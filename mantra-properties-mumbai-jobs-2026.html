Mumbai Recruitment Drive – 21 September 2026

Mantra Properties Walk-In Drive 2026

Mantra Properties is conducting a Walk-In Drive in Mumbai for experienced engineering and construction professionals. The recruitment drive is scheduled for 21st September 2026, Monday, from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The opportunity is intended for professionals who have relevant experience in civil contracting firms and are looking to explore engineering, project management, planning, commercial, quality, safety, procurement, stores and architectural career opportunities.

The available positions cover multiple important functions involved in construction and project execution. Candidates with experience in project execution, planning, cost management, quality control, purchasing, billing, safety, stores and architectural operations can review the requirements below and attend the walk-in interview at the specified venue. The recruitment drive includes opportunities ranging from Engineer-level positions to Project Manager, Manager, Associate Manager and Senior Manager-level roles depending on the department and experience.

📅 Walk-In Date: 21st September 2026 (Monday)

⏰ Interview Time: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

📍 Location: Mumbai

📍 Venue: Mantra Marquis, MHB Colony Road, Jogeshwari-Vikhroli Link Road, Sarvodaya Nagar, Jogeshwari East, Mumbai – 400060.

Important Eligibility Note:
Only experienced candidates from civil contracting firms are eligible to attend this walk-in drive. Candidates should carefully check the experience requirement of their preferred department before attending.
